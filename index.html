FurkAI YouTube Publisher

FurkAI YouTube Publisher is a private automation tool that prepares videos and uploads them to YouTube channels owned by its operator, Furkan Hatipoğlu. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

The tool asks for permission to upload videos to, and read basic performance statistics of, the operator's own YouTube channels only.
